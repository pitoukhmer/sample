# Ultimate Guitar: Pricing, Subscription Tiers, Paywall Design, Trial Mechanics, Conversion Funnel and Category Benchmarks (as of October 2026)

**Research-method note for the writer.** Nearly every primary domain (ultimate-guitar.com, help.ultimate-guitar.com, apps.apple.com, play.google.com, trustpilot.com, revenuecat.com, adapty.io, screensdesign.com, paddle.com, guitarchalk.com, truthinadvertising.org, mu.se, wikipedia.org) was blocked by the research sandbox's egress proxy, so full-page fetches failed. All findings below come from search-engine result snippets/summaries of those pages, which means (a) exact quotes are short, (b) dates on cached App Store snapshots are often unclear, and (c) several price points are **UNVERIFIED** and flagged as such. Treat every price as "observed on date X by source Y," not as a canonical list price. Ultimate Guitar (UG) runs near-constant promotions and appears to show different prices to different cohorts, so no single "current price" exists.

## Key Question 1: Current price points by region and platform, lifetime deals, Black Friday, student pricing, family plan

### Takeaway
UG does not have one price; it has a lattice of SKUs (Pro, Pro+, Pro+Courses, Pro+Courses+Sing, Practice, Sing, Courses, Songbook) whose list prices and promo prices differ by storefront, region, cohort and date. The most consistently observed 2025–2026 anchors are: web list price roughly **$99/yr or $25/mo**, promoted at "80% off" to roughly **$20–$40/yr**; US App Store bundle "UG Pro + UG Courses + UG Sing" at **$9.99/mo / $59.99/yr**; and iOS IAP SKUs clustering at **$29.99–$49.99/yr** and **$9.99–$12.49/mo**. No lifetime plan, student plan or family plan was found from any official source.

### Inferences
- The $99/yr "list" price functions as an anchor for a perpetual "80% off" offer (80% off $99.99 ≈ $20; observed promo prices of $22.99, €25, $29.99, $39.99 fit a 60–80% discount band). The realised annual price for most buyers is probably $20–$40, and the $99.99 figure mostly surfaces at auto-renewal, which is consistent with the complaint pattern ("unexpected $99 charges").
- iOS SKUs are priced lower than web list (Pro+ $39.99–$49.99/yr) presumably because Apple's price tiers and store-review constraints make a $99-then-80%-off dance harder; this suggests web is the higher-ARPU channel with more aggressive discount theatre.
- Multiple parallel SKUs (Pro, Pro+, +Courses, +Sing, Practice, Songbook) let UG upsell and also create the "I didn't know I had a second subscription" complaint seen on Trustpilot.

## Key Question 2: What is gated behind the paywall vs free (Pro/official tabs, chord variations, Simplify, autoscroll, offline, no ads, backing tracks, courses, Sing)

### Takeaway
Free = large user-contributed text tab/chord catalogue with ads and upgrade interstitials, plus offline favourites. Pro = licensed "Official" tabs, interactive Pro (Guitar Pro-style) tabs with playback/backing tracks, auto-scroll/smart-scroll, transpose, chord library/progressions, a beginner course and ad removal. Pro+ adds a "Let's Play"/Practice Mode layer; Courses and Sing are separate subscriptions bundled on iOS. Several features users remember as free (Transpose, Simplify, auto-scroll, printing) have migrated behind the paywall over time.

### Cited Findings

**Official descriptions**
- UG help center ("What is Ultimate Guitar Pro subscription?"): "Upgrade to the UG Pro for an enhanced experience: Access a Beginner Course developed for the UG app. Access official tabs for accurate representation," plus "auto-scroll and smartscroll functionality, the transpose feature to adjust the key of the tabs, and access to chord library and chord progressions" — [UG Help 6741560](https://help.ultimate-guitar.com/en/articles/6741560-what-is-ultimate-guitar-pro-subscription)
- UG help center ("Guide to Ultimate Guitar's premium services"): "Practice Mode is only available together with the Let's Play feature, so you'll need to be a subscriber to UG PRO or UG PRO+ to access it," and "Practice service is provided in the app only!" — [UG Help 6741293](https://help.ultimate-guitar.com/en/articles/6741293-guide-to-ultimate-guitar-s-premium-services)
- A separate "Ultimate Guitar Practice subscription" help article exists (article id 14588372), describing microphone-based listening for Chords tabs; "the Practice option is currently not available for Official Tabs on Android devices" — [UG Help 14588372](https://help.ultimate-guitar.com/en/articles/14588372-what-is-ultimate-guitar-practice-subscription)

### Inferences
- The free product is deliberately degraded over time (Transpose, Simplify, auto-scroll, print, playback) to push conversion; UG justifies gating via licensing royalties. This "feature-clawback" pattern is the dominant source of loyalty-loss complaints, distinct from the billing complaints.
- "Pro+" appears to be Pro plus the Let's Play/Practice layer (microphone feedback), but no official side-by-side exists in retrievable sources.
- If AppGoblin's 68% ad share is even directionally right, the free tier is a meaningful ad business, which explains why UG keeps a large free catalogue rather than going hard-paywall.

### Gaps

- No source on whether "chord variations" or offline Pro tabs are tier-specific.

## Key Question 3: Onboarding flow, quiz, trial offers, annual vs monthly push, when the paywall appears, win-back

### Takeaway
Mobile onboarding is a ~14-step quiz (instrument, genres, age) that lands directly on a three-tier paywall featuring a discounted "Welcome offer" plus monthly and yearly plans with a 7-day free trial; dismissing it is reported to trigger a secondary discount. The trial is attached to the annual plan (the source of most "charged $99/€40 after trial" complaints). Cancellation is self-serve but must be completed to a final confirmation step; cancelling a trial ends access immediately, while cancelling a paid plan keeps access to period end; refunds are never automatic. A class action (June 2025) alleges inadequate auto-renew disclosure and hard cancellation.

### Cited Findings

**Onboarding and paywall sequence**
- ScreensDesign teardown: "Onboarding steps: 14," and "the app presents a three-tier subscription paywall immediately after the onboarding quiz, highlighting a discounted 'Welcome offer' alongside standard monthly and yearly plans with a 7-day free trial"; "Bypassing the initial paywall triggers a secondary special offer granting '40% off forever.'" — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/apps/ultimate-guitar-chords-tabs/). **Caveat:** a second search could not re-confirm the "40% off forever" phrase for UG; ScreensDesign uses near-identical "$40 off forever" language for the app Bend in its paywall-examples article — [ScreensDesign paywall examples](https://screensdesign.com/articles/mobile-app-paywall-design-examples-2026/). Treat the dismissal-offer detail as plausible but unverified.
- Paddle "Fix That Funnel" episode on UG (web funnel): calls the onboarding "notably brief compared to competitors like Blinkist or Headway"; quiz covers "instrument, genres, and age" — [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/studios/shows/fix-that-funnel/ultimate-guitar)
- Appllama captured "48 captured screens across onboarding, paywall and core tabs" — [Appllama](https://appllama.io/apps/357828853/ultimate-guitar-chords-tabs)
- TechRadar reviewer tested "the week-long free trial offer" and wrote that "the week-long trial is shadily attached to the expensive yearly subscription" — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/websites-apps/ultimate-guitar)

**Cancellation and refund mechanics (official)**
- Web cancellation path: log in → Subscriptions → select plan → "Cancel trial and benefits"; subscriptions started in the mobile app must be cancelled via the website or via Apple/Google — [UG Help 6749057](https://help.ultimate-guitar.com/en/articles/6749057-website-how-do-i-cancel-my-ultimate-guitar-pro-subscription-or-discontinue-the-free-trial)

- Terms of Service: "all subscriptions require a commitment for a minimum period(s) of time," with cancellation "effective at the end of your then-current plan" — [UG ToS](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/about/tos.htm)

**Win-back / re-engagement**
- Lapsed user offered a returning-user rate of 25 EUR/year (anecdote; channel unspecified) — [JustinGuitar Community](https://community.justinguitar.com/t/ultimate-guitar-price-increase/386865)
- Trustpilot reviewer bought Pro after "a promotional email," sought a refund under EU withdrawal rules; UG's community manager replied and the company later issued a full refund — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ultimate-guitar.com)
- Muse Group lists a "Head of Monetization" role via its investor's careers portal, indicating a dedicated monetization/pricing function — [Francisco Partners careers](https://careers.franciscopartners.com/companies/muse-group-2-d371613d-bc8d-47cd-b632-5eda45332da3/jobs/45340992-head-of-monetization)

**Legal**

(June 2025)"; allegations include "failing to adequately disclose the terms of subscriptions – including that consumers who sign up for free trials are automatically enrolled in subscriptions – and making it difficult to cancel"; status "Pending" (as listed) — [Truth in Advertising class-action tracker](https://truthinadvertising.org/class-action/ultimate-guitar/)

### Inferences
- The paywall is "soft-hard": the app is usable free, but the post-quiz paywall, trial-on-annual default, re-engagement gate after a week of inactivity, and 30-second interstitials make the free path deliberately frictional. This matches RevenueCat's finding that hard-paywall apps achieve ~5x the trial-to-paid of freemium (see KQ4), i.e., UG is harvesting hard-paywall economics while keeping a freemium catalogue for SEO/ads.
- The combination of (a) trial defaulting to annual, (b) "80% off" countdown framing, (c) cancellation requiring a final confirmation step, (d) no automatic refunds, (e) separate SKUs (Songbook, Sing, Courses) silently coexisting, and (f) stored card details users say they cannot delete, is exactly the fact pattern alleged in the 2025 class action and is the primary reputational risk.

### Gaps
- No verified quote containing "no way to cancel during active period"; the closest official language is the ToS "minimum period" commitment and the help-center "access until end of paid period." Flag this phrase as unverified.
- No source on whether UG sends a pre-renewal reminder email; one Trustpilot reviewer says they were told a reminder would be sent and it "never arrived" — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.ultimate-guitar.com)
- No documentation of push-notification win-back cadence or email sequences.
- Could not view the actual paywall screens; tier names on the 3-tier mobile paywall are unknown beyond "Welcome offer / monthly / yearly."

## Key Question 4: Benchmarks for free-to-paid, trial-to-paid, churn, ARPU, LTV in music-education / hobby-learning apps

### Takeaway
Category benchmarks (RevenueCat 2025/2026, Adapty 2026) put Education apps at ~6.5% download-to-trial, ~28% same-day conversion (lowest of any category), D14 RPI ~$0.30 vs $0.23 all-category median, 12-month LTV ~$45, and a +50% LTV premium for trial starters. Across categories, year-one retention medians are ~28% for annual and ~8% for monthly plans; 17–32-day trials convert best (~42–46%) but most apps are shortening trials to ≤4 days; hard paywalls convert ~5x better than freemium (10.7% vs 2.1% D35 trial-to-paid) at similar year-one retention.

### Cited Findings

**RevenueCat — State of Subscription Apps 2026 (and Education cut)**
- Education RPI: "Business ($0.31 D14) and Education ($0.30 D14) follow Health & Fitness," vs a $0.23 all-category D14 median — [RevenueCat 2026 Education](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)
- Education has the lowest Day-0 conversion at 28.5%; categories "like Education and Health & Fitness, where 7-day trials are common, show increased conversion at the 7-day mark" — [RevenueCat 2026 Education](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)
- Download-to-trial: Education 6.5%, among the stronger categories behind Health & Fitness — [RevenueCat 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)
- Trials of 17–32 days convert at a median 42.5% vs 25.5% for trials under 4 days; share of apps using trials ≤4 days rose from 42.1% (2025) to 46.5% (2026) — [RevenueCat 2026 benchmarks blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)

## Key Question 6: Documented user complaints about billing, cancellation, price increases, loss of features

### Takeaway
Complaint volume is high and consistent across Trustpilot (~2,000 reviews, "Average" 3.2–3.7), PissedConsumer (141 reviews), App Store/Google Play reviews and forums, clustering into four themes: trial-to-annual auto-enrolment and surprise $99/€40 charges; cancellation friction and refund refusals; perpetual "80% off" countdown pricing that changes daily; and formerly free features (Transpose, Simplify, auto-scroll, print, certain tabs) moved behind the paywall. A June 2025 federal class action formalises the first two themes. UG replies to ~98% of negative Trustpilot reviews and sometimes refunds after escalation.
