# Ultimate Guitar competitive landscape (2026): chord/tab apps, guitar-learning subscriptions, AI entrants, and open-source building blocks

Research date: 2026-10-09. Method: ~45 web searches; direct page fetches (WebFetch) were blocked by the network proxy for nearly every domain (musicradar.com, digitalmusicnews.com, mu.se, chordify.pr.co, semrush.com, musicbusinessworldwide.com, jamorigin.com, bridgemena.com, startupresearcher.com), so most facts below come from search-result snippets of those pages rather than full-page reads. Numbers from third-party trackers (AppGoblin, App Pricing Lab, subger, Semrush, Similarweb, Lazyweb, Clodura) are flagged as ESTIMATES. Prices change often and vary by storefront/region; treat every price as "as observed on the cited date".

## Key Question 1: Per-competitor profile — price, free tier, differentiation, traction, ratings, strengths, weaknesses (comparison matrix)

### Takeaway
The market splits into three clusters: (a) ad/freemium chord-and-tab catalogs priced at ~$40–120/yr (Ultimate Guitar Pro ~$40/yr list, Songsterr $9.99/mo, Chordify ~$54/yr, Cifra Club PRO); (b) listening/feedback curriculum apps priced at ~$120–200/yr (Yousician Premium+ $119.99/yr, Simply Guitar $119.99–199.99/yr, Fender Play ~$149.99/yr, Gibson $129.99/yr, JustinGuitar app $64.99–129.99/yr); and (c) video-lesson libraries at $149–250/yr list but heavily discounted (TrueFire, Guitar Tricks, Pickup Music). No competitor publishes audited revenue; the only hard financial datapoints are Yousician's $50M recurring revenue (2020), JoyTunes/Simply's ~$100M ARR with Simply Guitar at ~$10M ARR (2021), and Moises/Music AI's $40M Series A with 50M registered users (Jan 2025).

### Inferences
- UG's list price (~$40/yr) is the cheapest "catalog" subscription in the set; its upside is in moving up-market with listening/AI features where Yousician/Simply charge 3x as much — but reviews show those features are also the most complained-about.
- Moises' aggressive consumer price ($36–40/yr for stems + chords) plus 50M users positions it as the most credible threat to UG's "play along with any song" use case rather than to UG's catalog.
- Lifetime SKUs are rare (Chord ai, JustinGuitar reseller); the market has converged on annual subscriptions with deep seasonal promos (TrueFire $79–99 vs $249 list; Guitar Tricks <$100).

- Jam Origin MIDI Guitar 3 price, Chordu, E-Chords traffic, Planning Center Music Stand price and SongSelect 2026 tiers not found.
- No 2025–2026 financial or headcount news for Yousician or Simply was found.

## Key Question 2: Which apps have real-time listening/feedback and how accurate/reliable are they per reviews?

### Takeaway
Yousician, Simply Guitar, Gibson, Ultimate Guitar Practice Mode (PRO, mobile), Yousician's "June" AI teacher, and newer entrants (Chordie AI, SoundGate, GuruAI) all listen via the microphone; reviews consistently flag false negatives (correct note marked wrong), chord/timing detection problems, and sensitivity to tuning and background noise. No independent quantitative accuracy benchmark of any consumer app was found.

### Cited Findings
- Ultimate Guitar Practice Mode (launched Jan 31, 2024): records the session, uses ML, gives post-practice (not strictly real-time) feedback on pitch and rhythm; acoustic and electric; iOS-only at launch; PRO/PRO+ only — [Muse Group](https://mu.se/post/ultimate-guitar-launches-practice-mode). A third-party piece says it grades "pitch, timing and accuracy", highlights missed notes and can loop them — [Unite.ai](https://www.unite.ai/how-ai-is-changing-guitar-practice-for-the-next-generation-of-learners/). Another review notes it auto-scrolls notation by detecting where you are in the song — [Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/apps/com-ultimateguitar-tabs100).
- Yousician: official support article attributes recognition problems to tuning with a different tuner, background noise, guitar effects, and mic permission — [Yousician support](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/46277332028817-Guitar-sound-recognition-issues). ## Key Question 3: Automatic chord generation from audio and lyrics licensing

### Takeaway
Chordify, Moises, and Chord ai generate chords from arbitrary audio (and YouTube/streaming links) and therefore do not depend on user-contributed tabs; historically they showed no lyrics because lyrics are copyrighted text requiring publisher licenses, which Chordify solved via a LyricFind partnership. Moises gates full-song chord detection behind Premium (free = first minute); Chord ai gates "more precision" behind Pro.

### Cited Findings
- Chordify "automatically extracts chords from your favorite songs" from uploaded audio or YouTube/SoundCloud/Deezer links — [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/?p=23269). Chordify now shows lyrics through a LyricFind partnership ("integration of licensed lyrics") and added live chord detection — [MusicRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/chordify-lyrics-live-chord-detection); [Chordify press](https://chordify.pr.co/164897-chordify-android-app).
- Licensing context: Musixmatch (the other major licensed-lyrics aggregator) raised money in 2010 specifically "to make online lyrics legal" and markets a "100% licensed lyrics database" — [TechCrunch 2010](https://techcrunch.com/2010/07/29/musixmatch-aims-to-make-online-lyrics-legal-with-a-new-e400k-round); [Musixmatch API catalog](https://library.wiremock.org/catalog/api/m/musixmatch.com/musixmatch-com). No source links Chordify to Musixmatch.
- Riffstation's 2018 withdrawal of web/mobile versions was tied to Fender "working on a paid service involving labels and publishers" — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/software/riffstation/about) — i.e., rights clearance was the stated obstacle for an audio-chord product tied to streaming catalogs.
- Moises: free users get AI chord detection for the first 1 minute of each song; Premium unlocks all songs — [Moises blog](https://moises.ai/blog/latest/advanced-chord-detection/). A third-party reviewer's unverified accuracy figure: 85–90% — [Chartlex](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/marketing/moises-ai-review-2026).
- Chord ai: free version "enables the recognition of basic chords"; Pro adds precision, playlists, drive backup — [App Pricing Lab (Google Play)](https://apppricinglab.com/app/google_play/com.chordai).
- Songscription ("Shazam for sheet music", AI transcription) raised $5M led by Reach Capital (Nov 2025) — [press clipping](https://songscription.rockpaperscissors.biz/artist/21268/press_clips/33294/white).

### Inferences
- The strategic reason auto-chord apps avoided lyrics: chord symbols alone are generally treated as non-infringing analytical data, whereas lyrics are literary works requiring publisher licenses (the business Musixmatch/LyricFind exist to sell). Chordify paying LyricFind implies the lyrics feature is a licensed cost line, likely justified by premium conversion. (Inference; no Chordify statement of rationale was found.)
- UG's user-submitted chord+lyrics model carries its own publisher-licensing obligations, which the auto-chord apps avoided by not hosting lyrics — a structural cost difference.

### Gaps
- Date and terms of the Chordify–LyricFind deal, and whether lyrics are premium-only, not confirmed (MusicRadar/Chordify pages blocked).
- No published accuracy claims from Chordify or Chord ai; Moises' 85–90% is a single blogger's figure.

## Key Question 6: Open-source / API building blocks — chord recognition, pitch, stem separation, benchmarks, APIs for sale

### Takeaway
Open-source chord estimation has plateaued: Chordino/NNLS-Chroma reaches ~80% chord-symbol recall, autochord 67% on Billboard, and the field "still has not exceeded 90%" on simple labels, with 2025 conformer/Mamba models (Chordformer, BMACE, Poltronieri et al.) reporting a "glass ceiling" partly due to annotator subjectivity; stem separation is strong (HT-Demucs ~7.5 dB SDR without extra data, ~9.0 dB with 800 extra songs on MUSDB18-HQ); monophonic pitch (CREPE) is solved while polyphonic note transcription (Basic Pitch) is usable but benchmark-dependent. Commercial APIs: Klangio (chords/strums, free tier → volume/on-device), Music.ai (50+ modules, pay-as-you-go/professional/enterprise, chord module unconfirmed), Fadr (API with $10/mo Plus), Replicate-hosted CNN-LSTM chord model.

### Cited Findings
**Chord recognition**
- Chordino (NNLS-Chroma, Vamp plugin): ~80% chord symbol recall, "still considered state-of-the-art" (2015); the top MIREX 2014 system (Khadkevich) had no public code — [ISMIR 2015 paper](https://www.ismir2015.uma.es/articles/271_Paper.pdf).
- "Current approaches still have not exceeded 90% accuracy on simple chord label prediction tasks" — [ISMIR 2021 LBD](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2021/latebreaking/000051.pdf).
- Evaluation-metric choice (root / majmin / sevenths / inversions) strongly changes MIREX rankings — [Pauwels & Peeters](https://researchportal.ip-paris.fr/en/publications/evaluating-automatically-estimated-chord-sequences/); [ISMIR 2014](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2014/paper/000320.pdf).
- madmom: CNN deep-chroma + CRF major/minor chord recognizer; ships MIREX ACE evaluation measures; no published accuracy number in docs — [madmom chords docs](https://madmom.readthedocs.io/en/v0.16/modules/features/chords.html); [madmom evaluation](https://madmom.readthedocs.io/en/v0.16/modules/evaluation/chords.html).
- autochord (TensorFlow, 25 classes maj/min/N): 67.33% chord accuracy, root WCSR 74.77, majmin WCSR 70.62 on 100 Billboard test songs — [ISMIR 2021 LBD](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2021/latebreaking/000008.pdf); [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/autochord).
- 2025 SOTA direction: Chordformer (conformer, large vocab, arXiv 2502.11840); BMACE (Mamba, "comparable to SOTA with fewer parameters"); Poltronieri/Serra/Rocamora ISMIR 2025 (conformer; decomposed root/bass/note training; argue the field hit a "glass ceiling" partly from annotator subjectivity) — [arXiv 2509.01588](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01588v1); [ISMIR 2025 poster](https://ismir2025program.ismir.net/poster_268.html); training on artificially generated audio — [arXiv 2508.05878](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.05878). Extended-chord accuracy drops sharply vs triads due to training-set imbalance — [SBCM 2025](https://sol.sbc.org.br/index.php/sbcm/article/view/43948). Billboard (890 songs) is harder than Isophonics/Beatles — [Oracles for ACE](https://core.ac.uk/works/133553239).
- Hosted open model: Replicate "triadmusic/chord-detection-cnn-lstm" (audio in, JSON/.lab out) — [Replicate](https://replicate.com/triadmusic/chord-detection-cnn-lstm/versions/91156c10a898fed71e86508bf11442219cd4d00024d8f72193ca9e6be36c6da1/api); npm "chord-recognition" package — [npm](https://npmjs.com/package/chord-recognition).

**Pitch / transcription**
- CREPE: CNN F0 estimator, monophonic only; torchcrepe port; CREPE Notes segments contours into notes (monophonic) — [Papers with Code](https://paperswithcode.com/paper/crepe-a-convolutional-representation-fo2); [torchcrepe](https://pypi.org/project/torchcrepe/0.0.1); [crepe-notes](https://pypi.org/project/crepe-notes); [CREPE Notes paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.08884).
- Spotify Basic Pitch: open-source polyphonic audio→MIDI; benchmarked by third parties with mir_eval (note correct if pitch matches and onset within ±50 ms), thresholds often tuned — [Builder.io library](https://best-of-web.builder.io/library/spotify/basic-pitch); [arXiv 2509.12712](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.12712); compared vs CREPE Notes and Melodyne on jazz bass (onset-only) — [FiloBass](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.02023). Basic Pitch's own F-measures not retrieved (see Gaps).
- Essentia: broad MIR toolkit; described as more comprehensive than Basic Pitch's note-only scope — [Builder.io](https://best-of-web.builder.io/library/spotify/basic-pitch). No Essentia chord-accuracy figure found.

**Stem separation**
- HT-Demucs on MUSDB18-HQ: mean SDR 7.52 (drums 7.94, bass 8.48, other 5.72, vocals 7.93) without extra data; 9.00 with 800 extra songs; SCNet reaches 9.0 without extra data — [SCNet paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.13276). Hybrid Demucs: 7.72 (MUSDB18) / 7.68 (HQ) — [Papers with Code](https://www.paperswithcode.com/paper/hybrid-spectrogram-and-waveform-source). Third-party median SDR: htdemucs vocals 8.53 / drums 10.01 / bass 9.78 / other 6.42; htdemucs_ft 9.19 / 10.11 / 10.38 / 6.34 (StemSplit model card, own benchmark) — [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/StemSplitio/htdemucs-ft-pytorch); toolkit collection — [HF collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/StemSplitio/music-source-separation-toolkit-2026).

**Commercial APIs**
- Klangio API: transcription, source separation, beat tracking, chord and strum recognition, model per instrument; async job (URL or Base64 in, poll status, download); "chord_recognition_extended" adds key detection; free tier, volume contracts, on-device licensing — [Oomol Klangio skill](https://oomol.com/es/skills/oo-klangio/source.md).
- Music.ai (Moises' B2B): API + SDKs + desktop client; all accounts have API access; Pay-as-you-go / Professional / enterprise volume pricing (no public figures); 50+ modules; a directory lists stems, BPM, beat detection, lyrics transcription etc. but not chords — [Music.ai FAQ](https://music.ai/docs/faq/); [devtoollab](https://devtoollab.com/ai-tools/moises).
- Fadr: API access included in Plus plan at $10/month — [iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/products/fadr/). StemSplit launched a stem-separation API — [StemSplit](https://stemsplit.io/blog/stemsplit-api-launch.md).

### Inferences
- A pragmatic 2026 pipeline for a UG-style "chords from any audio" feature: Demucs/htdemucs_ft (or SCNet) to isolate harmonic stems → madmom/Chordino or a Chordformer-class model for chords → Basic Pitch for melody/bass lines → CREPE for single-note practice feedback. Expect ~80–85% majmin accuracy on pop and materially worse on extended chords; human correction (Chordify's crowd edits, Serenade-style human-in-the-loop) remains necessary — [Serenade](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.11165).
- Buying vs building: Klangio is the only vendor found that explicitly sells chord+strum recognition with on-device licensing; Music.ai sells the surrounding stack but chord availability is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- No MIREX ACE results table for 2023–2025 surfaced (MIREX chord task appears dormant; recent papers benchmark on Isophonics/Billboard instead). Exact WCSR numbers for Chordformer/BMACE/Poltronieri not retrieved.
- Basic Pitch's official F-measure (Bittner et al., ICASSP 2022) and Essentia chord accuracy not retrieved. Klangio and Music.ai per-call prices not published in search results.
