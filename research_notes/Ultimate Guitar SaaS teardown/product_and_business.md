## Key Question 1: Company ownership, headquarters, founding, revenue, employees, funding, acquisitions

### Takeaway
Ultimate Guitar (UG) was started in October 1998 by Eugeny Naidenov in Kaliningrad, Russia, is operated through Ultimate Guitar USA LLC (San Francisco-registered) and since April 2021 has been the founding brand of Muse Group, a Limassol, Cyprus-headquartered holding that also owns MuseScore, Audacity, StaffPad, Hal Leonard, Audio.com, MuseHub and Tonebridge. Francisco Partners took a minority growth stake in 2023 to fund the Hal Leonard acquisition and was fully bought out on June 3, 2026 (financed by J.P. Morgan-arranged credit plus cash); Naidenov remains majority owner/CEO. No audited revenue figures for UG or Muse Group are public; headcount is only available as third-party estimates (roughly 200-420).

### Inferences
- The June 2026 buyout of Francisco Partners with debt rather than new equity implies Muse Group generates enough cash flow to service J.P. Morgan-arranged senior credit; this is consistent with a profitable subscription + ads + Hal Leonard print business, but no revenue number is public.
- Muse Group's "500M annual" figure (2026) vs "300M annual visitors / 40M accounts for UG+MuseScore" (2023) and "400M" (mid-2025) suggests the headline audience claim is a cross-brand reach number that grew mostly via the Hal Leonard and Audio.com additions, not a UG-specific MAU.
- Hal Leonard ($300M+ revenue ESTIMATE, ~650 staff) is likely the largest revenue line in the group, so group-level revenue estimates would be dominated by print/physical publishing rather than UG subscriptions.

### Inferences
- AppGoblin's "$100K+/month, 68% ads" iOS revenue estimate is almost certainly a severe undercount of UG's subscription revenue, since (a) UG deliberately routes subscriptions through its own web checkout (Paddle) to avoid store fees, and (b) the app ranks #7 top-grossing in US Music (March 2026), which implies far more than ~$32K/month IAP.
- Company "30M MAU" likely counts web + app; third-party web-visit estimates of 60-77M/month are compatible with that if many visitors return several times per month.
- Indonesia and the Philippines being top-3 traffic markets implies a large free, ad-monetized long tail with low subscription ARPU, while the US/UK/Canada likely drive most paid conversions.

## Key Question 3: Feature list by tier (Free vs Pro vs Pro+Courses+Sing), including the AI coach

### Takeaway
Free users get the user-submitted chord/tab catalog (1.4M-2.1M tabs), favorites with offline access, personal tabs, Shots and community features, interrupted by ads and Pro upsells. Pro (web + app) unlocks Official (staff-made, licensed) tabs with backing tracks and synced lyrics, Pro interactive tabs, autoscroll/SmartScroll, transpose, Simplify, chord library, tuner/metronome tools, and Practice Mode ("your very own AI-powered music coach," mobile only, mic-based pitch/rhythm tracking). Courses (1,500-2,500 lessons) and Sing (20K+ interactive karaoke-style songs with feedback) are separate app-only subscriptions bundled as "UG Pro + UG Courses + UG Sing" at $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr on the US App Store; Pro-only web pricing is ~$24.99/mo or ~$99.99/yr list, routinely discounted up to 80% (~$65/yr sale) after a 7-day trial. A newer "AI chords" feature (May 2026) generates chord charts from a YouTube link.

## Key Question 4: Content pipeline and licensing (UGC, moderation, Official tabs, publisher/lyrics licensing)

### Takeaway
UG runs a two-track catalog: ~2M user-submitted tabs reviewed by volunteer/staff moderators (typically approved within 48 hours, with a 3-day revision window and a star-rating system) and a staff-produced "Official" tier (22.5K-29K tabs with backing tracks and synced lyrics) that is licensed and Pro-gated. Rights come from direct agreements with "thousands of publishers" (Sony, EMI, Peermusic, Alfred, Hal Leonard, Faber, Music Sales) plus an April 2010 Harry Fox Agency blanket deal covering lyrics display, title search and tab download/print; songwriters are paid when tabs are displayed. Since Dec 2023 Hal Leonard is a sister company, with its licensed arrangements slated for "digitized, interactive formats" on Muse platforms. No LyricFind/Musixmatch/MusicNotes relationship was found.

### Cited Findings
- "All Official Tabs are created by the Ultimate Guitar team, and all non-official tabs are submitted by users from all over the world"; tabs "are reviewed by the tab's moderators before they become publicly available" — [UG Help: Tabs](https://help.ultimate-guitar.com/en/articles/6741306-tabs)
- Approval SLA: tabs following the Tablature Guide are "typically reviewed and approved within 48 hours"; "Tab Administrators, Moderators, or experienced contributors will provide detailed feedback"; "3-day window to revise"; if no response, moderators may edit and approve; support team does not review tabs — [UG Help: approval time](https://help.ultimate-guitar.com/en/articles/6749103-how-long-does-it-usually-take-for-my-tab-to-be-approved)
- Corrections: anyone can use "suggest correction" on a live tab; it enters the same review queue (user forum description) — [UG Forum: Getting a tab approved](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/forum/showthread.php?t=1780832)
- Ratings: star rating is an arithmetic mean of votes (older UG blog); ratings are meant to reflect tab quality only — [UG Blog: New rating system](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/article/blog/view?id=8); [UG Forum: Tab rating system](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/forum/showthread.php?t=1623214)
- Community criticism of moderation: rejections for "lack of formatting" without specifics; tabs must be "absolutely perfect to be approved" — [UG Forum: administrative review](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/forum/showthread.php?t=1696903)
- Site rules/community guidelines exist as a blog page — [UG site rules](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/article/blog/site-rules)
- Publisher deals: UG "has signed license agreements with thousands of publishers, including Sony, EMI, Peermusic, Alfred, Hal Leonard, Faber and Music Sales, through which the songwriters receive compensation for the display of the tabs" — [Wikipedia: Ultimate Guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultimate_Guitar)
- Harry Fox Agency: "On April 10, 2010, Ultimate Guitar entered an additional licensing agreement with Harry Fox Agency," including "rights for lyrics display, title search and tablature display with download and print capabilities"; HFA-represented publishers opt in — [Wikipedia: Ultimate Guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultimate_Guitar)
- UG's own licensing explainer: publishers "issue the licenses and collect the money"; "UG has a license agreement with The Harry Fox Agency through which ten[s] of thousands of other publishers can grant rights to Ultimate Guitar to allow users to use and share guitar tablature" — [UG Blog: licensing](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/article/blog/licensing)
- Hal Leonard tabs "are generally described as professionally transcribed"; platform-wide licensing is preferred by publishers because enforcing against individual user uploads is impractical — [Guitar Chalk: licensed tabs](https://www.guitarchalk.com/are-licensed-tabs-more-accurate/)
- Hal Leonard integration intent: Hal Leonard's licensed arrangements "offered in digitized, interactive formats on their platforms"; combined 5.5M+ items — [mu.se: Hal Leonard](https://mu.se/post/hal-leonard-joins-muse-group)
- User-tab-to-Pro migration complaints: forum users say "the good tabs are being changed to 'pro' status" and "half the tabs are gone, I assume updated to pro status" — [UG Forum: accurate tabs behind paywall](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/forum/showthread.php?t=2114822); [UG Forum: confused about paywall](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/forum/showthread.php?t=1801574)
- Songbooks: a SevenString forum thread (~early 2025) says new "songbooks... took certain tabs down and locked them behind another paywall," and that anything available through "Sheet Happens" publishing is taken down even if a user tab predated the official version — [SevenString: Ultimate Guitar is dead](https://sevenstring.org/threads/ultimate-guitar-is-dead.368845/)
- Older third-party tool "Ultimate-Guitar-Hack" for downloading Guitar Pro files including Official tabs (~4 years old) indicates Pro tabs are delivered as Guitar Pro-format interactive files — [GitHub](https://github.com/noahmaranesi/Ultimate-Guitar-Hack)
- Music/digital-media casebooks (2022-2026 editions) use Ultimate Guitar's licensing as a teaching case — [Open Casebook 2026](https://opencasebook.org/casebooks/16851-music-digital-media-spring-2026/as-printable-html/4/)

### Inferences
- The catalog strategy is to let UGC (free, ad-supported, SEO-heavy) capture search demand, then progressively replace or gate the most-searched songs with staff-made Official/Pro versions; forum reports of user tabs vanishing when a licensed version appears are consistent with publisher-exclusivity terms (e.g., Sheet Happens) rather than accidental removal.
- Owning Hal Leonard gives Muse Group in-house rights to a large print catalog, reducing third-party licensing cost for Official tabs and Courses, though no public evidence yet confirms Hal Leonard arrangements have shipped inside UG as of 2026.

### Gaps
- No source describes royalty rates, payout totals, or how HFA/publisher money is split; no evidence of LyricFind, Musixmatch or MusicNotes relationships was found.
- No dated 2024-2026 announcement of Hal Leonard content appearing on UG was found.
- The current count of volunteer vs paid moderators, and the "contributor" rank system, could not be retrieved.
