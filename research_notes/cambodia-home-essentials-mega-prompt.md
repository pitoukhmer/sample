## 1. What the research found (and how sure we are)

Confidence labels: SEEN = visible in your own photos and videos. SOURCED = from a published source, link in Section 6. UNVERIFIED = needs checking before any money moves.

**The inspiration site.** homefixcambodia.com did not load from the research environment (the domain did not resolve), so nothing here is taken from the live site. A Cambodian business directory describes Home-Fix as a DIY and home improvement chain operating since 1993 with stores in Singapore, Malaysia and Cambodia, located on Mao Tse Toung Boulevard (SOURCED, directory page of unknown age). Third party marketplace listings under its name show Bosch power tools, Karcher cleaners and a PowerPac steamboat grill at $49.90 (SOURCED). So it is a hardware and DIY retailer first. Your idea takes only the appliance and daily household slice of that range, which is a different and faster moving business.

**The supply you filmed.** A Phnom Penh wholesaler stocked in depth with (SEEN):

One retail price point seen on Khmer24: Sokany 5L air fryer, new, $45. No wholesale prices are confirmed. Every cost figure in any plan must come from a written quote from this wholesaler.

**The market.** Social commerce is the main channel: Facebook first, then TikTok and Telegram (SOURCED, US trade.gov and DHL). About 11.65 million Facebook users and 9.96 million TikTok users were reported for 2024 (SOURCED, no 2026 figure found). Cash on delivery is still roughly a quarter of online orders and KHQR bank QR is widely used; exact payment shares conflict between sources, so treat them as UNVERIFIED. Appliance sales have historically been concentrated in Phnom Penh (SOURCED, old article).

**Delivery cost.** Published rates cluster around $1.00 to $1.50 inside Phnom Penh and $1.50 to $2.00 to provinces for small parcels, with same day COD cash return offered by some couriers (SOURCED, but several rate pages are old). Bulky items such as folding beds and 6 unit cartons will cost more. Get current rate cards. UNVERIFIED for 2026.

**Rules.** The E-Commerce Law requires a permit or licence to sell online, with an exemption reported for individuals and sole proprietors below the small taxpayer turnover line, given as KHR 250 million a year, about $62,500 (SOURCED from law firm notes of mixed age). Small taxpayer patent tax was KHR 400,000 for 2025 and VAT is 10 percent (SOURCED). Household electrical products fall under mandatory safety standards administered by the Institute of Standards of Cambodia (SOURCED, partly from a 2003 text). Confirm all of this with the Ministry of Commerce, the General Department of Taxation and an accountant. UNVERIFIED for your case.

**Risks worth naming early.**
1. Brand authenticity. Philips boxes sit beside low cost brands in the same stock. Ask for proof of authorised supply before advertising any global brand, or do not list it.
2. Origin wording. Brand names that include a country (SGP "Singapore", Osaka "Japan") do not prove where the product was made. Never state origin beyond what the box and import paperwork support.
3. Electrical safety. Heating blenders, kettles, fryers and gas stoves can injure people. Check plug type, voltage, cable gauge and a working test before dispatch.
4. Warranty. A reseller who promises warranty carries it. Get the wholesaler's swap terms in writing first.
5. Commodity pricing. Everyone can buy the same Sokany box. Price alone will not keep you ahead; trust, speed and after sales will.

## 3. The master mega prompt (same for all four tools)

```
ROLE
You are a retail and social commerce operator who has built and run home-based online
shops in Cambodia. You write for a solo founder who moves fast and will execute this
personally. Be practical, numerical and honest. No motivational language.

MISSION
Write a business plan and an operating playbook for a home-based online store in
Cambodia selling small home appliances and daily or urgent household necessities, with
a small physical showroom or pickup corner at home for buyers who want to see or test
before paying. The goal is a business that is ahead of other small sellers in this
sector within 12 months, measured by repeat purchase rate, delivery speed, return rate
and net profit, not follower counts.

SCOPE
In scope: small kitchen electrics (blenders, heating blenders, juicers, kettles, rice
cookers, air fryers, electric pans), personal care electrics (clippers, dryers,
shavers), fans and cooling, lighting and power (LED bulbs, rechargeable lights,
extension cords, power strips), portable gas stoves, food storage and lunch boxes,
bottles and flasks, basic kitchenware, cleaning tools, folding beds, and tools for
micro vendors (price computing scales, ice crushers, food machines).
Urgent need items deserve their own analysis: what a family needs today because
something broke, the power went out, a baby arrived, a relative moved in, school
started, floods came or a festival is near.
Out of scope: large appliances needing installation, power tools and construction
hardware, food, medicine, cosmetics, anything needing a professional licence.

KNOWN FACTS (treat as given, do not contradict without evidence)
- The founder has filmed a Phnom Penh wholesaler carrying SGP, Sokany, DR International,
  Geemy, Gemei, VGR, KEA, Flame and Green Star stock plus unbranded lunch boxes,
  bottles, stainless ware, scales, LED bulbs and folding beds. Philips boxes were also
  present; authorised supply is not confirmed.
- One retail reference: Sokany 5L air fryer listed new at $45 on Khmer24.
- No wholesale prices are confirmed yet.
- Selling in Cambodia runs mainly through Facebook pages and live video, TikTok,
  Telegram chat ordering and Khmer24 listings. Payment is cash on delivery or KHQR and
  bank app transfer (ABA, ACLEDA, Wing and others).
- Indicative delivery cost: about $1.00 to $1.50 in Phnom Penh and $1.50 to $2.00 to
  provinces for small parcels. Unverified for the current year.
- Reported rules to verify: e-commerce permit required with an exemption for sole
  proprietors under about KHR 250 million annual turnover; patent tax; 10 percent VAT
  above threshold; mandatory safety standards for household electrical goods under the
  Institute of Standards of Cambodia.
- Founder inputs: [PASTE SECTION 2 HERE]

HARD RULES
1. Never invent a price, cost, fee, market size, conversion rate or legal requirement.
   Every number must carry one tag: [SOURCE: link or document], [FOUNDER DATA], or
   [ASSUMPTION: reason, and how to verify in Cambodia within one week].
2. Do not promise or imply income. Give three scenarios (low, base, high) with the
   assumptions that drive each and the break even point.
3. Do not claim any product is original, authorised, certified or made in a specific
   country unless a document proves it. Flag every brand where authenticity or origin
   is unclear and say what proof to request.
4. Do not name real competitors' private data, real customers or real suppliers beyond
   what is public. Do not write fake reviews or testimonials.
5. Flag anything that needs a licence, tax registration or safety check, state who to
   confirm it with, and do not present your reading of the law as final.
6. If a founder input is missing and it changes the answer, ask up to 7 questions first,
   then proceed with clearly labelled assumptions if told to continue.
7. All money in USD with KHR where customers will see it (4,000 to 4,100 KHR per USD,
   state the rate used). Customer-facing copy in natural Khmer with English beneath.

DELIVERABLES, IN THIS ORDER
A. One page verdict: is this worth doing with the stated capital, the three biggest
   reasons it could fail, and the single best starting wedge.
B. Demand map: who buys (urban renters, new households, garment and office workers,
   provincial families, students, micro vendors, landlords), what triggers the
   purchase, what they pay now and where. Separate everyday demand from urgent demand.
C. Product selection: score at least 40 candidate products on demand frequency,
   urgency, margin potential, delivery cost against price, breakage and return risk,
   safety risk, video demonstrability, seasonality and competition. Output a ranked
   table, then a launch range of 15 to 25 products split into traffic items, profit
   items and basket builders. Say what not to sell and why.
D. Unit economics template per product: landed cost, packaging, delivery subsidy, COD
   refusal rate, return and warranty reserve, ad cost per order, payment fees, net
   margin. Leave cost cells blank for the founder's wholesale quotes and show the
   minimum selling price formula.
E. Sourcing plan: stage 1 buying from Phnom Penh wholesalers in small lots or
   pre-order without holding stock; stage 2 negotiating credit, consignment or
   exclusivity; stage 3 direct import from China, Thailand or Vietnam with landed cost
   method and import duties to verify. Include a supplier question list and a quality
   check routine for each incoming carton.
F. Home operation design: stock layout for a small room, a 4 to 10 m2 showroom or
   pickup corner, testing bench for electrical items, opening hours by appointment,
   safety and security for receiving strangers at home, signage, Google Maps listing.
G. Sales channels: role of each of Facebook page, Facebook live, TikTok and TikTok
   Shop, Telegram channel and bot, Khmer24, Google Maps and a simple website or
   catalogue. Say which to start with and which to delay, with reasons.
H. Content system: 30 day calendar, 10 short video scripts that demonstrate products in
   real Cambodian homes, a live selling run sheet, product photo standard, and a
   message reply script covering price, delivery, warranty, bargaining and COD
   refusal.
I. Fulfilment and after sales: courier choice by zone, same day Phnom Penh cut-off
   time, packing standard for fragile items, test video before dispatch, COD
   reconciliation, returns, a written warranty policy the business can afford.
J. The ahead-of-everyone playbook: at least 12 specific advantages a small seller can
   build that typical Cambodian page sellers lack, for example tested before
   dispatch video sent to every buyer, a 7 day swap promise, spare parts and
   accessories, bundles for new households and new vendors, urgent 2 hour delivery
   tier, repeat buyer list in Telegram, landlord and dormitory bulk offers, honest
   wattage and electricity cost information, Khmer usage guides, AI assisted chat
   reply and inventory tracking. For each: cost, difficulty, how fast a competitor
   could copy it, and how to measure it.
K. Legal and compliance checklist: business and tax registration path for a solo
   seller, e-commerce permit position, product safety, consumer protection, trademark
   for the shop name, record keeping. Each item with the office to confirm with.
L. Financial plan: startup budget at three capital levels ($500, $2,000, $5,000 or the
   founder's figure), monthly fixed costs, 12 month low, base and high scenarios,
   cash flow with COD delay, break even order count, stock turnover target.
M. 90 day action plan by week, with a test budget, kill criteria for products that do
   not sell, and the 8 numbers to track every week.
N. Risk register: counterfeit and unsafe goods, price wars, COD refusals, supplier
   stockouts, courier loss, page bans, home security, currency and seasonal swings.
   Each with an early warning sign and a response.
O. Open questions for the founder and a list of every [ASSUMPTION] in one table so
   they can be verified one by one.

STYLE
Plain language. Tables for anything comparative. Short paragraphs. No filler, no hype.
Where you are unsure, say so and say how to find out.
```

## 4. Tool specific wrappers (paste after the master prompt)

## 5. How to combine the four answers

Run ChatGPT, Kimi and Gemini first, save each answer as a file, and give all three to Claude Code as inputs so it builds the final plan and model. Then send the finished plan back to one of the others with this closing prompt:

```
Act as a sceptical investor and a Cambodian retail veteran. Attack this plan. List the
10 weakest assumptions, any number without a source, any claim that could mislead a
customer, and the cheapest test that would prove or kill each assumption within two
weeks. Do not rewrite the plan. Only find what is wrong with it.
```

Where two tools disagree on a fact, trust neither until a wholesaler quote, a courier rate card or an official page settles it.
