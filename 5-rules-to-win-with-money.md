# 5 Rules to Win With Money (explained like you're 10)

From Dave Ramsey's talk in the video. His promise: do all five and you will win with money. Skip any one and you will lose. These are simple, but "simple" is not the same as "easy", so the trick is to set them up once and then just follow the system.

---

## Rule 1: Write your plan down (a budget)

**Like you're 10:** Imagine playing a board game with no rules and no board. Nobody wins by accident. A budget is just the rule sheet for your money. Before the month starts, you write down every dollar coming in and tell every dollar where it goes. If it's not written, it's not a plan, it's a wish.

**Guideline:** On the last day of each month, write next month's plan. Income minus all spending must equal zero (every dollar has a job). Check it once a week.

**Sample monthly plan (fill in your own numbers):**

| Line | Planned | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Money coming in (salary, business pay) | $1,500 | |
| Giving | $100 | |
| Saving / investing | $200 | |
| Rent / home | $400 | |
| Food | $250 | |
| Electricity, water, phone, internet | $100 | |
| Transport (moto, fuel, Grab) | $80 | |
| Debt payments | $200 | |
| Family support | $100 | |
| Fun / eating out | $50 | |
| Everything else (clothes, medicine, gifts) | $20 | |
| **Left over** | **$0** | |

The last line must be zero. If money is left, give it a job (usually debt or savings). If you are negative, cut a line until it's zero.

---

## Rule 2: Get out of debt

**Like you're 10:** Your paycheck is your superpower for building wealth. Debt is like letting other people hold your superpower. Every month you send money to the bank, the moto shop, the phone company. That money built their tall buildings and their furniture stores. When you have no payments, guess what you have? Money!

**Guideline:** List every debt, smallest to biggest. Pay minimums on all of them, and throw every extra dollar at the smallest one. When it dies, roll that payment into the next one. This is the "debt snowball". Stop borrowing while you do it.

**Sample debt list (smallest first):**

| Debt | Owed | Minimum payment | Extra this month | Paid off date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone installment | $120 | $20 | $80 | Dec 2026 |
| Friend loan | $300 | $30 | $0 (until #1 is done) | |
| Moto loan | $900 | $60 | $0 | |
| Bank loan | $2,500 | $90 | $0 | |

Rule of the game: when the phone is paid off, the $100 you were sending there goes to the friend loan on top of its $30. Repeat until the list is empty.

## Rule 3: Live on less than you make

**Like you're 10:** If you get 10 candies and eat all 10, you have zero candies tomorrow. If you eat 8 and keep 2, you start building a pile. The verse he quotes (Proverbs 21:20) says the wise person keeps stores of food and oil in the house, but the foolish person eats everything they have. If you spend everything, you are a fool. Harsh, but true.

He also says: don't wait for wages to grow or for someone to fix your life. Nobody is coming. Decide today that you will control the things you can control.

**Guideline:** Pick a percentage you spend and a percentage you keep. Set it once. Spend only from the "spend" part. Never touch the "keep" part for normal life.

**Sample split (start here, tighten later):**

| Bucket | Share | On $1,500 income |
|---|---|---|
| Spend (rent, food, bills, fun) | 80% | $1,200 |
| Keep (debt payoff now, then savings/investing) | 20% | $300 |

Practical trick: the day money arrives, move the "keep" share to a separate account or wallet before you spend anything. What you cannot see, you do not spend.

---

## Rule 4: Save and invest

**Like you're 10:** How do rich people get rich? They save. Then they let the saved money grow by investing it. 100% of people who never save never have any money. That sounds obvious because it is. Saving is not what's left at the end of the month. It is a line in the budget you pay first, like rent.

**Guideline:** Three steps in order. First a small emergency fund so a surprise does not become new debt. Second, finish killing debt (Rule 2). Third, a bigger emergency fund, then invest a fixed amount every single month.

**Sample savings ladder:**

| Step | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Starter emergency fund | $500 to $1,000 | Save fast, then stop and attack debt |
| 2. Debt free | $0 owed (except maybe a house) | Debt snowball from Rule 2 |
| 3. Full emergency fund | 3 to 6 months of expenses | Build it in the budget, then leave it alone |
| 4. Invest every month | 15% of income | Automatic, same day each month, boring and steady |

Keep step 1 and 3 money somewhere safe and reachable, not in something that goes up and down. Step 4 is long-term money you don't touch for years.

---

## Rule 5: Be outrageously generous

**Like you're 10:** This is the fun one. Once you have a plan, no debt, you live on less than you make, and you save, you will have extra. Give some of it away, on purpose and sometimes randomly. Pay for the stranger's meal, help the family who needs rice this month, tip big. Generous people are the happiest people with money, and being generous proves money doesn't own you.

**Guideline:** Put giving in the budget as a fixed line (it is the first line in the sample above) and keep a small "random generosity" pocket for surprise moments.

**Sample giving plan:**

| Type | Amount | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Planned giving | 10% of income, or a fixed amount you choose | Pagoda, church, a family you support, a charity |
| Random giving | $20 a month pocket | Pay for someone's lunch, extra tip, help a neighbor |

---

## The whole system on one line

Write the plan, kill the debt, spend less than you earn, save and invest first, give generously. Do it every month. That is the entire game.

## Monthly routine (10 minutes)

Last day of the month: write next month's zero-based budget. Payday: move the "keep" share out first. Every Sunday: check actual vs planned, adjust one line. When a debt dies: roll its payment to the next debt. Once a year: raise the "keep" percentage by a few points.
