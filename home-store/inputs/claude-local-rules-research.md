# Local rules, couriers and local context: web research

Prepared 2026-11-28 for the Prek Ta Meak (Khsach Kandal, Kandal) home-appliance and household-goods shop.

**How to read this file**

- This is research, not legal or tax advice. Every row names the office to confirm with.
- Method: web search in English and Khmer. Direct page fetching was blocked in this session for every site tried (tax/law-firm sites, courier sites, news sites), so findings rest on search-result extracts, not on full-text reading of the source. Treat each figure as "reported by the source named", and re-open the link before relying on it.
- Source type is marked: **OFFICIAL** (government or the company itself), **SECONDARY** (law firm, accounting firm, news, directory, blog). Where only secondary sources exist, that is said.
- "Not found" means not found in this session, not that no rule exists.
- Exchange rate used for conversions: KHR 4,000 per USD (plan range is 4,000 to 4,100).

**Reading for this shop:** if first-year turnover stays under KHR 250 million (about USD 62,500, or roughly USD 5,200 a month), the secondary sources agree no e-commerce permit is needed. Above that, a permit (KHR 400,000 / 2 years) would apply. No change to this threshold for 2026 was found.
**Confirm with:** Ministry of Commerce, Business Registration Department / e-commerce licensing desk (or Kandal Provincial Department of Commerce).

**Reading for this shop:** the KHR 250 million line (about USD 62,500 a year) is the single number that decides e-commerce permit, tax registration, patent tax and VAT together. Below it, public statements say no tax, no patent tax, no returns; above it, the shop becomes a small taxpayer with patent tax KHR 400,000, monthly returns and 10% VAT. Stock capped at USD 10,000 turning every two weeks would imply turnover well above USD 62,500 a year, so the plan should assume the threshold is crossed in the base and high scenarios and price with VAT in mind. No new 2026 threshold change was found; the only 2026 item found is the extension of voluntary-registration incentives.
**Confirm with:** GDT Kandal Provincial Tax Branch, or GDT call centre 1277 (number shown on tax.gov.kh).

## HANDOVER SUMMARY

1. Method limit: search extracts only; no page could be fetched. Nearly all legal findings are SECONDARY (law/accounting firms, news). Not legal advice.
2. E-commerce permit: sole proprietors under the small-taxpayer bracket (KHR 250M, about USD 62,500/yr) are exempt [Acclime; Conventus/Lexology 2022; BNG 2021]. If required: KHR 400,000, 2 years [DFDL on Joint Prakas 315, 2021]. Fine up to KHR 10M [Phnom Penh Post 2021]. Confirm: MOC Business Registration.
3. Tax classes: small taxpayer = KHR 250M to 1,000M turnover for commerce (Prakas 009, Jan 2021) [PwC; DFDL]. Below 250M: government statements say no tax, no patent tax, no returns [Khmer Times, Aug 2024 and later]; no GDT text found.
4. Patent tax: KHR 400,000 a year for small taxpayers, due 31 March [Crowe; Emerhub; PwC].
5. 2026 change: GDT extended voluntary-registration incentives for informal MSMEs through 2026 (patent tax exemption 2 years reported) [CJBI 2 Jan 2026; MISTI doc]. Sector eligibility for retail unverified. No 2026 threshold change found.
6. VAT 10%; applies once in the self-declaration regime (same KHR 250M entry point) [Deloitte; Grant Thornton]. Plan implication: USD 10,000 stock turning fortnightly exceeds USD 62,500/yr, so assume threshold is crossed in base/high cases. Confirm: GDT Kandal branch / 1277.
7. Free optional informal-economy registration via CAM-IE app or banks [informal.digitaleconomy.gov.kh, OFFICIAL; launched 16 Dec 2024].
8. Consumer protection (Law of 2 Nov 2019): misleading acts are unlawful even if unintentional; Prakas 95 (12 Apr 2024) covers online ads, penalty up to KHR 80M [Lexology]; information-standards Prakas (2021) requires Khmer labels, retailers to guarantee goods as advertised, written warranty with period and conditions, and public disclosure if sold without warranty [ASEAN consumer portal / FAOLEX text]; limiting guarantee liability is an unfair term (Prakas 0067, 2022) [DFDL]; Khmer-first advertising (sub-decree 4 Nov 2022) [Tilleke]. No statutory repair/refund rule found. Confirm: CCF, MOC.
9. Electrical safety: mandatory CS standards (IEC 60335 based) date from a 2004 Prakas; registration/ISC mark is the importer's duty [NTR; TUV SUD]. Sub-Decree 254 (Aug 2023) adds energy labels for AC, then fans, rice cookers, LEDs; rollout status unverified [Tilleke]. Ask wholesaler for importer documents. Confirm: ISC (MISTI), MME.
10. Scales: instruments sold in Cambodia need NMC initial verification [National Trade Repository, OFFICIAL]. Reseller licence and penalties not found. Confirm: NMC / provincial MISTI office.
11. Butane stoves/canisters: no retail rule found; MME lists a 29 Apr 2025 Prakas delegating LPG can/cylinder storage permits to district administrations (content unread). Confirm: Khsach Kandal district office, fire police.
12. NSSF: applies from 1 employee; register within 30 days, worker within 3 days [Ogletree; DFDL; Lexology]. Employer 0.8% + 2.6% + 2% pension; employee 2%. 2026 change: wage floor KHR 600,000 / ceiling 1,500,000 from Nov 2026 (Joint Prakas 234/26) [Acclime; HBS Law]. Pension rises to 8% total from Oct 2027. Employer cost at USD 150 to 180 wage: about USD 8 to 10/month (calculated).
13. Minimum wage: garment sector only, USD 210 (2026), USD 212 from 1 Jan 2027 [KPMG Nov 2026; The Star 30 Sep 2026]. No statutory minimum for retail found; planned USD 150 to 180 is below local factory pay (retention risk).
14. Trademark: DIPR, MOC; official fees reported inconsistently at about USD 105 to 120 per class (KHR 420,000 to 460,000); 6 to 18 months; 10-year term [Emerhub; ASEAN Briefing; Kaizen 2017]. Affidavit-of-use details unverified.
15. Couriers: no 2026 rate card found for any courier. J&T: "from KHR 5,000", free pickup, COD cap USD 500, COD needs VIP registration, 2 to 3 days [J&T Cambodia pages]. VET: COD cash returned within 2 days, insurance 1%, 10+1 membership, nationwide to commune level [VET site]. CE Express 2023: USD 1.00 same province under 3 kg, USD 1.50 PP to province, USD 2.00 cross-road [company notice]. Larryta, Capitol, Grab: nothing usable. Founder's USD 1.00 to 2.00 range is plausible but unverified for 2026 and for Khsach Kandal origin.
16. Population: Preaek Ta Meak commune 12,413 (2019), 11,182 (2008); Khsach Kandal district 140,878 (2019) [citypopulation.de]. Preaek Ampil 12,258; Preah Prasab 8,441.
17. Access: shop is at the head of National Road 8 (about 124 to 129 km east to Ponhea Kraek) at the Prek Tamak Mekong bridge (1,060 m, opened 2010/11), linking to NR6A [Wikipedia; Kampuchea Thmey]. Public sources say bridge is about 40 km from Phnom Penh vs founder's 30 km.
18. Local demand anchors: a private school branch at the bridge; garment factory in an SEZ in the district; vehicle assembly plant ground-breaking 26 Jul 2026; no worker counts or market data found [Fresh News Nov 2026; Khmer Times; Better Cambodia 2026].
19. Development: Koh Khsach Tonlea bridge studies (2026); new Phnom Penh to Arey Ksat Mekong bridge announced Oct 2026; Phnom Penh to Bavet expressway target 2027 [Construction & Property; Khmer Times].
20. Wholesale: Orussey Market is the documented wholesale cluster for household goods, hardware and electronics (ground floor; Street 182 plastics) [travel blog; directories]. Central Market is retail. Olympic not confirmed for appliances. No authorised-distributor data for the founder's brands.
21. Google Business Profile: walk-in shop qualifies as a storefront; Google assigns the verification method, usually a continuous video showing street, signboard and proof of control; review up to 5 business days [Google Help].
