Scope: the "special assignment" only (retail prices, seller patterns, seasonality, buyer complaints and gaps).

## 0. How to read this file

- **Method.** Web search only, in Khmer and English. Khmer24, Facebook and TikTok could not be opened directly from this environment (khmer24.com does not resolve here), so every price below comes from what the search index returned for a page, not from a live page view.
- **Date seen.** All rows were seen in the search index on 2026-11-28. The date the seller posted the listing is mostly unknown; where a clue exists it is given. Treat every price as an asking price of unknown age, to be re-checked by phone or Telegram before use.
- **Evidence tiers.**
  - **A** = the price appears in the listing's own page title, with the listing URL.
  - **B** = the price was reported by the search tool's reading of the page (shop page, category page or listing body). The URL is the page it came from. Less certain than A; verify before relying on it.
- **All prices are Cambodian listings in USD** unless a row says otherwise. Prices from other countries were found and discarded (Egypt, Nepal, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, Kenya, Australia) and are not used anywhere in this file.
- **Sellers.** Shops are named only where the listing shows a business name. Listings by individuals are shown as "individual seller".
- **Not verified here:** authenticity, origin, warranty or certification of any product. "Made in Thailand", "Japan" and similar wording in listings are seller claims only.
