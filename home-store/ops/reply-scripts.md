# Customer reply scripts (Khmer and English)

The Khmer here was written by an AI. A native speaker must read and correct it before it is used with customers. Replace every [bracket] with real details. Do not use the warranty lines until the wholesaler has confirmed swap terms in writing.

## 1. Price question
សួស្តីបង! [ឈ្មោះទំនិញ] តម្លៃ [តម្លៃ]$ (ប្រហែល [តម្លៃរៀល]៛)។ ហាងយើងសាកល្បងដំណើរការមុនប្រគល់ជូន ហើយធានាប្តូរថ្មីក្នុង ៧ថ្ងៃ បើខូចពីរោងចក្រ។
Hello! [Product] is $[price] (about [riel] riel). We test every unit before handing it over, and swap it within 7 days if it has a factory fault.

## 2. Delivery question

Where are you located? Inside Prek Ta Meak commune we deliver today. For other provinces we send by courier, about [n] days, delivery fee $[fee].

## 3. Can I see it first?
បានបង! អញ្ជើញមកមើលនិងសាកល្បងផ្ទាល់នៅហាង ជិតផ្សារ [ឈ្មោះផ្សារ]។ បើបងនៅឆ្ងាយ ខ្ញុំថតវីដេអូសាកល្បងទំនិញផ្ញើជូនមុនវេចខ្ចប់។
Of course. Please come and try it at the shop near [market name]. If you are far away, I will film the item working and send you the clip before packing.

## 4. Warranty question
ធានាប្តូរថ្មី ៧ថ្ងៃ បើខូចពីរោងចក្រ សូមរក្សាប្រអប់និងប័ណ្ណធានា។ មិនធានាលើករណីធ្លាក់បែក ចូលទឹក ឬប្រើខុសពីការណែនាំ។
7 day swap for factory faults. Please keep the box and the warranty card. Drops, water damage and misuse are not covered.

## 5. Bargaining
តម្លៃនេះជាតម្លៃល្អបំផុតរបស់យើងហើយបង ព្រោះរួមទាំងការសាកល្បង និងធានាប្តូរ។ បើបងយក ២ មុខឡើង ខ្ញុំជូន [កាដូ/បញ្ចុះ]។
This is already our best price, and it includes testing and the swap promise. If you take two items or more, I can add [gift or discount].

## 6. Is it original? Where is it made?
ខ្ញុំប្រាប់ត្រង់ៗ៖ នេះជាម៉ាក [ម៉ាក] ដូចបង្ហាញលើប្រអប់។ ព័ត៌មានប្រទេសផលិត សូមមើលលើប្រអប់ផ្ទាល់។ យើងសាកល្បងគ្រប់គ្រឿងមុនលក់។
To be straightforward: this is the [brand] brand as shown on the box. For the country of manufacture, please read the box itself. We test every unit before selling.
(Never say original, authorised, or made in a named country unless a document proves it.)

I am sorry about this. Please send a short video showing the problem and a photo of the warranty card. If it is within 7 days, we will swap it right away.
