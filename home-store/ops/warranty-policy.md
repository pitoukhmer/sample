# Warranty policy (draft)

Use only after the wholesaler confirms in writing: the 7 day swap for faulty stock, and which brands or models get 1 to 2 months. The shop must never promise longer than the supplier covers. The Khmer was written by an AI and needs a native speaker's review. Have the wording checked against Cambodian consumer protection rules.

## Card text

**ប័ណ្ណធានា / Warranty card**

ហាង / Shop: [ឈ្មោះហាង]  ទូរស័ព្ទ / Phone: [លេខ]
ទំនិញ / Item: ____________  លេខស្ទីកគ័រ / Sticker no.: ________
ថ្ងៃទិញ / Date: ________  ទូរស័ព្ទអ្នកទិញ / Buyer phone: ________

១. ទំនិញនេះត្រូវបានសាកល្បងដំណើរការមុនប្រគល់ជូន។
1. This item was tested and working before handover.

២. ប្តូរថ្មីក្នុងរយៈពេល ៧ថ្ងៃ បើមានបញ្ហាពីរោងចក្រ។
2. Swap for a new unit within 7 days if there is a factory fault.

៣. សូមយកទំនិញ ប្រអប់ គ្រឿងបន្លាស់ និងប័ណ្ណនេះមកជាមួយ។
3. Please bring the item, box, accessories and this card.

៤. មិនធានា៖ ធ្លាក់បែក ចូលទឹក ឆេះដោយសារប្រើភ្លើងមិនត្រឹមត្រូវ ប្រើខុសពីការណែនាំ ឬជួសជុលដោយខ្លួនឯង។
4. Not covered: drops, water damage, damage from incorrect power supply, misuse, or self repair.

៥. ទំនិញខ្លះមានការធានាយូរជាងនេះ៖ ______ (ហាងបំពេញ)។
5. Some items carry longer cover: ______ (filled in by the shop).

## Shop rules behind the card
1. Buyer far away: accept a video of the fault first, then arrange return by courier. The shop pays the return fee when the fault is confirmed.
2. Swap first, argue with the supplier second. Claim the unit back from the wholesaler on the next delivery, inside their window.
3. No identical unit in stock: offer another model of equal value or a refund.
4. Log every claim: date, model, brand, fault, outcome, whether the supplier replaced it.
5. Any report of burning, shock or injury: stop selling that model the same day and tell the wholesaler.
