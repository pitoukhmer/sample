# Shop checklists

## Incoming carton check (same day the delivery arrives)
1. Count cartons against the order message. Note shortages on the delivery slip before the driver leaves.
2. Open each carton. Check model number, colour and quantity against the invoice.
3. Look at every box: crushed corners, broken seals, signs of repacking.
4. Electrical items: check the plug type fits local sockets, the label says 220 to 240 V, the cable is not thin or damaged.
5. Plug in and run every unit for at least one minute. Kettles: boil once. Blenders: run with water. Clippers: run and listen. Bulbs: light each one.
6. Film 10 seconds of each unit working. Save the clip under the model name and date.
7. Smell or heat at the plug, sparks, rattles, leaks: set aside, label FAULTY, message the wholesaler with the clip the same day.
8. Write the serial number or a shop sticker number on each unit and in the stock sheet.
9. Price label on the shelf in riel and dollars before the item goes on display.
10. Update the stock sheet and the amount owed to the wholesaler, with the invoice date and the day 14 payment date.

## Sale and handover (walk-in)
1. Run the item in front of the buyer.
2. Explain one safety point (for example, never run a blender empty, never cover a fryer vent).
3. Fill in the warranty card: date, model, sticker number, buyer phone.
4. Take payment, record cash or KHQR, give a receipt.
5. Invite the buyer to the Telegram channel.

## Packing for courier
1. Test clip sent to the buyer and seen.
2. Payment or deposit received and recorded.
3. Glass jars and lids wrapped separately, padding on all corners, box does not rattle when shaken.
4. Warranty card and a short usage note inside.
5. Label: name, phone, province, district, COD amount in riel and dollars.
6. Photo of the sealed parcel with the label. Record courier, tracking number and COD amount.
7. Mark the day the COD cash is due back and tick it off when received.

## Weekly stock and order routine
1. Evening before order day: count class A items, list anything sold out.
2. Check the amount owed to the wholesaler against the ceiling.
3. Order up to two weeks of sales for class A, one replacement for class B items sold, deposits-paid items for class C.
4. Mark items with no sale in 30 days for a price cut or bundle.
5. Fill in the WeeklyTracker sheet.

## Live selling run sheet (45 minutes)
1. Before: 8 to 10 items on the bench, prices written large, stock counted, assistant ready to answer comments.
2. Minutes 0 to 5: greet, say where the shop is, state the swap promise.
3. Minutes 5 to 35: one item every 3 to 4 minutes. Show it working, state watts and capacity, state the price, say how many are available, say how to order.
4. Minutes 35 to 45: repeat the three most asked items, answer questions, give the order cut-off for next day dispatch.
5. After: reply to every comment and message the same evening, record orders, note which items drew questions.
Do not use fake countdowns, invented stock limits or claims about origin or brand authorisation.
