# Meta Ads Playbook: Pitou AI, Pitou Foods, Bunna Store

Prepared 19 Nov 2026. Source: the Meta for Business "first ad" Reel you shared plus current Meta Ads practice. I could not transcribe the Reel's audio or open the facebook.com page from this environment, so nothing here is quoted from them. The Reel itself is Meta's generic small-business pitch (a creator talking about running his first ad, with street and laptop b-roll) pointing to the Advertiser Success Center. The useful part is the method below, adapted to your three businesses.

Every budget figure in this file is a starting test amount I am recommending, not a Meta price. Real costs per message or lead in Cambodia are unknown until you run the first test.

## 1. One-time setup (do once, shared by all businesses)

Create one Meta Business Portfolio (business.facebook.com) owned by you, then add each business as its own Facebook Page and Instagram account inside it. Give each business its own ad account so spend and results never mix. Add a payment card to each ad account (a Cambodian Visa or Mastercard debit card normally works; test with a small charge first). Turn on two-factor authentication on your personal profile, because losing that profile means losing every ad account.

Connect WhatsApp Business and Messenger to each Page, since in Cambodia most buyers want to chat before they pay. For anything with a website (Pitou AI), install the Meta Pixel and the Conversions API so Meta can see who actually signs up or buys. Without that, Meta optimises for clicks, which are cheap and mostly worthless.

Use Ads Manager, not the "Boost post" button. Boost is fine for a quick test, but Ads Manager gives you the right objective, placement control and proper reporting.

## 2. How Meta ads work now (the short version)

You pick one objective (Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App promotion, Sales) and Meta finds the people most likely to do that action. The current default is Advantage+: Advantage+ audience (broad targeting, Meta finds the buyers), Advantage+ placements (Facebook, Instagram, Reels, Stories, Messenger together) and Advantage+ creative (Meta auto-crops and tweaks your ad). For small budgets, leave audience broad within your location and let creative do the targeting. Your video and its first three seconds decide who Meta shows the ad to.

Each ad set goes through a learning phase. Meta's guideline is around 50 optimisation events per week for it to stabilise. With small budgets you will not reach that on purchases, which is why messages and leads are the right first objectives for you: they happen often enough for Meta to learn.

Do not edit a running ad set every day. Each significant change resets learning. Judge results after 5 to 7 days.

## 3. Creative rules (apply to every ad)

Vertical 9:16 video, 15 to 30 seconds, hook in the first 3 seconds, Khmer on-screen captions (most people watch muted), one clear offer, one call to action. Film on your phone; native-looking content beats polished ads on Reels. Make 3 to 5 versions per campaign that differ in the hook, not the whole video, and let Meta pick the winner. Keep the Page's recent posts active, because people check the Page before messaging.

Policy traps to avoid: no income or earnings promises (matters most for Pitou AI), no "you" statements that imply personal attributes ("Are you overweight?"), no before/after health claims for food, and for Pitou Foods never state or imply Halal certification anywhere in copy, visuals, hashtags or comments replies.

## 4. Bunna Store (most urgent: soft open 28 Nov, grand opening 5 Dec)

Goal: footfall and Messenger/Telegram followers in Prek Tameak and Ksach Kandal.

Campaign A, starting about 24 Nov: Awareness or Engagement objective, location set to a radius of roughly 5 to 10 km around the store pin, Advantage+ audience, ages 18+. Creative: the store build, family stocking shelves, the roadside price board with one big number, "Grand opening Saturday 5 December." Suggested test budget USD 3 to 5 per day.

Campaign B, 28 Nov to 12 Dec: Engagement objective with "Messages" as the conversion location (Messenger and WhatsApp). Ad offers something concrete, for example "Message us to get the opening-day price list" or a small free gift on the first visit. Suggested USD 5 per day. Every message is a lead your wife or daughters answer the same day.

After opening, keep one always-on Reel ad per week showing new stock and the price tiers, USD 2 to 3 per day. Track in the Daily Sales sheet how many customers say they saw the Facebook ad.

## 5. Pitou Foods

Goal: orders by chat (and resellers or shop partners if that is part of the model).

Campaign: Engagement objective, conversion location Messages (Messenger plus WhatsApp), location set to the delivery area only, Advantage+ audience. Creative: cooking and packing footage, close-ups of the food, price per pack and delivery area in the caption, a customer-style "unboxing" clip. Run 3 hooks: taste and texture, convenience (ready in X minutes), price or bundle. Suggested test USD 5 per day for 7 days, then double the winning ad and pause the rest.

Set up Messenger instant replies and saved responses with the menu, prices and delivery zones so you do not lose people at 10 pm. Add a retargeting ad set later for people who watched 50% of a video or messaged but did not order.

Reminder: no Halal claims, no health or medical claims. Prices in ads must match what you actually charge on the day.

## 6. Pitou AI

Goal: qualified leads (demo calls or sign-ups), not traffic.

Campaign: Leads objective using an Instant Form (stays inside Facebook, works far better on mobile in Cambodia than sending people to a website). Choose the "Higher intent" form type and add 2 or 3 qualifying questions (business type, team size, what they want automated) so you filter out curious clicks. If you sell by chat, use the Messages destination instead and qualify in Messenger. Suggested test USD 5 to 10 per day. Once the Pixel and Conversions API are live on your site and you have regular sign-ups, test a Sales or Leads campaign optimised for the website event.

Creative: screen recordings of the actual tool doing a task, a 20-second "before and after" of a workflow, founder-to-camera explaining one problem you solve. Do not promise income, savings figures or results you cannot verify, and do not name or show any client without written permission.

## 7. Weekly routine (30 minutes, Monday)

Check cost per result (message, lead, or form), not clicks or likes. Pause any ad that costs more than about twice your best ad's cost per result after it has spent roughly 3 times that cost. Move budget to the winner in steps of about 20%, not all at once. Add 1 or 2 new creatives per business per week, since ads fatigue in 2 to 4 weeks. Write down, for each business, spend, results, cost per result and actual sales that week. If messages come in but sales do not, the problem is the chat follow-up or the offer, not the ads.
