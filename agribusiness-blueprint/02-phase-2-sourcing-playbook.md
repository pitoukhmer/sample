## 2.2 Vetting Checklist: Fertilizer & Pesticide Manufacturers / OEM Distributors

Score each item 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (pass). **Must-pass items (marked ★) are automatic rejection if 0.** Minimum total to proceed: 26 out of 36.

### A. Legal & product legitimacy

- [ ] ★ Every product label shows a **MAFF registration number**. Photograph each one.
- [ ] ★ The registration number is checked against the MAFF/GDA list of registered agricultural materials (ask PDAFF if you cannot find it online) and matches the product name and company.
- [ ] ★ Label is in **Khmer** with nutrient composition (N-P-K % and micronutrients), usage instructions per crop, safety warnings, net weight/volume, manufacturer/importer, batch number, manufacturing date, expiry date.
- [ ] Supplier shows its own business registration and agricultural materials business license (photo).
- [ ] ★ No product on their list contains active ingredients that are banned or restricted in Cambodia (applies when you later add pesticides; ask PDAFF for the current banned list).

### B. Commercial terms

- [ ] Dealer discount of **at least 25%** off the recommended retail price at the $200 to $500 order tier.
- [ ] First order MOQ of **$500 or less**, or mixed-SKU orders allowed to reach MOQ.
- [ ] Written **retail price policy** (minimum advertised price) applied to all online dealers, so you are not in a price war with other resellers of the same brand.
- [ ] Return or exchange for damaged, leaking or expired units; buy-back of unopened stock within 60 days is a strong plus.
- [ ] Payment terms: 30 days on part of the order, or consignment for the first order.
- [ ] Will deliver to Phnom Penh or ship to provinces via bus/courier at agreed cost; drop-ship option.

### C. Product performance & support

- [ ] Can show field trial data or before/after photos with dates and locations.
- [ ] Gives you **2 farmer references** you can call. Call both. Ask: "Did you buy again? What changed in your crop?"
- [ ] Provides marketing support: Khmer brochures, product videos, an agronomist phone line for technical questions from your customers.
- [ ] Manufacturing date of stock offered is within the last 6 months; at least 12 months remaining before expiry.
- [ ] Storage at the supplier warehouse is dry, shaded, organized by batch.

### D. OEM / private-label readiness (score now, decide later)

- [ ] Offers OEM/private-label manufacturing.
- [ ] States the OEM MOQ, unit cost, label design process and lead time in writing.
- [ ] Clearly states **who holds the MAFF product registration** for a private-label product and who pays for registration, lab analysis, and field tests.

### Red flags (any one means walk away)

1. Labels only in Thai, Vietnamese or Chinese with a Khmer sticker added, and no MAFF number.
2. Price far below every other supplier with no explanation.
3. Claims like "doubles yield on any crop" or "replaces all other fertilizers."
4. Refuses to give an invoice or receipt.
5. Pushes you to sell pesticides without asking whether you are licensed.

## 2.3 Vetting Checklist: Local Processors / Artisans (Banana Flour, Lotus Root Powder, Cashews)

Score 0/1/2. **★ = must pass.** Minimum total to proceed: 30 out of 44.

### A. Hygiene & premises (check on site, Day 8)

- [ ] ★ Processing area is separate from sleeping areas, animals and cooking for the family.
- [ ] ★ Workers wear hair covers and wash hands; soap and clean water are available at the processing area.
- [ ] Floors, tables and equipment are clean; no visible mold, insects, rodents or droppings.
- [ ] Windows/drying areas are screened or covered; drying is in a solar dryer or covered rack, not open ground next to a road.
- [ ] Water source for washing is clean (well with lid, piped or treated).
- [ ] Raw materials stored off the floor, covered, dated.
- [ ] Finished stock stored in a dry, cool place away from fertilizers, fuel and chemicals.

### B. Product quality & shelf life

- [ ] ★ Each package shows **product name, ingredients, net weight, production date, expiry date, producer name, address, phone**.
- [ ] Any registration, certificate or recognition from a government body (for example MISTI/Ministry of Industry, provincial department, CamControl, or a One Village One Product program). Photograph it. Do not claim certifications they do not hold.
- [ ] Shelf life stated and plausible: banana flour and lotus powder usually around 6 to 12 months when properly dried and sealed; roasted cashews shorter unless vacuum or nitrogen-flushed (confirm with each processor).
- [ ] **Banana flour test:** dry, free-flowing, no lumps, no sour or musty smell, light cream to light brown color consistent across the 3 batches.
- [ ] **Lotus root powder test:** ingredient list says 100% lotus root (ask directly whether any cassava or other starch is added). Compare texture and color across batches. Mixed starch sold as pure lotus powder is a known risk; if they admit blending, it must be on the label.
- [ ] **Cashew test:** grade stated (whole vs splits/pieces); fewer than 5% broken in a whole-grade pack; no rancid or oily smell; no insect holes; even roasting color; salt level consistent. Open one pack after 7 days at room temperature to check crispness.
- [ ] Packaging is food-grade, sealed (heat-sealed or zip-lock with inner seal), not leaking powder, printable or labeled neatly.
- [ ] ★ Producer gives at least 3 units from **different production dates** for your consistency test.

### C. Capacity & commercial terms

- [ ] Monthly production capacity is at least 3x your Month 1 estimate (for example 300 pouches if you expect 100), so you can scale without switching supplier.
- [ ] Wholesale price gives you **30% or more** gross margin at the retail price you benchmarked on Day 2.
- [ ] Accepts one of: split payment, consignment (ផ្ញើលក់), or small MOQ of 20 to 30 units.
- [ ] Will agree to a retail price policy so their own shop and other resellers do not undercut online.
- [ ] Open to online exclusivity after a 60-day trial with a sales target.
- [ ] Can ship to Phnom Penh by bus/courier within 3 days of order.

### D. Story & content (this is what sells online)

- [ ] Allows you to film the process, the family, the raw material source (banana plantation, lotus pond, cashew orchard).
- [ ] Owner willing to appear in at least one video or give a short statement.
- [ ] Clear origin you can state truthfully: village, district, province.

### E. Export readiness (score now; required in Phase 5)

- [ ] Willing to add English labeling (ingredients, allergen: "Contains tree nuts (cashew)", net weight, best-before, producer address, "Product of Cambodia").
- [ ] Can provide a production/ingredient declaration letter for customs.

### Red flags

1. Refuses a site visit.
2. No dates on packages, or dates handwritten after you ask.
3. Different taste, color or texture between batches with no explanation.
4. Health claims printed on the package ("cures," "treats," "prevents disease"). You cannot repeat these, and they signal careless compliance.

## 2.4 Vetting Checklist: Durian & Fruit Orchard Owners

Score 0/1/2. **★ = must pass.** Minimum to proceed: 28 out of 40. For any pre-harvest deposit (ម៉ៅចម្ការ), the minimum is 34.

### A. Land, ownership & access

- [ ] ★ Farmer **owns the land** (land title or soft title shown) or, if renting, has written consent from the landowner for selling the harvest and for any pre-harvest contract.
- [ ] Location pinned on Google Maps; road passable by truck in rainy season.
- [ ] Phone with Telegram or Messenger; can send photos and videos daily during harvest.
- [ ] Respected in the village: ask 2 neighbors or the village chief (មេភូមិ) about the farmer's honesty with past traders.

### B. Orchard data (write it in the `Farms` tab)

- [ ] Number of bearing trees, tree ages, varieties (for durian: local Kampot/Kep variety, Monthong, other named varieties; for oranges/pomelo: variety names).
- [ ] Yield for the last 3 seasons (kg per tree or total kg) and the prices traders paid each season.
- [ ] Harvest window for this farm (start and end dates last season).
- [ ] Who bought last season and on what terms (cash at farm, deposit, pre-buy of whole orchard).

### C. Quality & maturity standards

- [ ] ★ **Durian maturity:** farmer harvests only mature fruit (knows days after flowering per variety; uses stem/tap test) and does not cut immature fruit to catch high early prices. Ask directly: "How do you know a durian is ready?"
- [ ] **Durian dry matter (optional but strong):** if a lab or buyer test is available, mature durian is commonly judged by dry matter content of the pulp (for reference, Thailand's export minimums are around 32% for Monthong; confirm the standard you adopt for each variety).
- [ ] **Brix for oranges/pomelo** measured with your refractometer (OPS.3). Target for oranges: **10 °Brix or higher (EST, set your own standard after testing 3 farms)**. Record the reading for each sample lot.
- [ ] Blind cut test: ask the farmer to pick 3 fruits they call "Grade A." Open all 3 in front of them. Check for worm/borer damage, unripe or watery flesh, rot at the stem.
- [ ] Farmer accepts **grading by your written standard** (Schedule C of the MOU) and accepts photo/video inspection on arrival.

### D. Chemical safety

- [ ] Keeps spray records (product, date) or can state them; uses MAFF-registered products only.
- [ ] Respects pre-harvest intervals on the label (no spraying close to harvest).
- [ ] No ripening chemicals applied to fruit sold to you as naturally ripened.

### E. Partnership fit

- [ ] Wants a long-term buyer, not just the highest price this week.
- [ ] Willing to let you film the orchard, the family and the harvest, and use the farm name in marketing.
- [ ] Will reserve a fixed quantity per week for you during the season.

### Red flags

1. Refuses the blind cut test.
2. Cannot say last season's yield or price.
3. Already took a deposit from another trader for the same trees this season.
4. Land ownership unclear or disputed.

## 2.5 Word-for-Word Scripts (Khmer + English)

**How to use:** read the Khmer aloud. Keep the tone warm and respectful. Use **បង** for someone near your age, **ពូ / មីង** for older farmers, **លោក / លោកស្រី** for company managers. The English line under each block is the meaning, not a script to read.

---

### Script A: Local Processor (banana flour / lotus root powder / cashews), become their online sales partner

**A1. Phone opening**

> ជម្រាបសួរបង! សុំទោសដែលរំខានបងបន្តិចណា។ ខ្ញុំបានឃើញម្សៅចេករបស់បងនៅលើហ្វេសប៊ុក ហើយខ្ញុំបានទិញសាកមួយកញ្ចប់រួចហើយ ឆ្ងាញ់ ហើយវេចខ្ចប់ស្អាតណាស់។ ខ្ញុំកំពុងចាប់ផ្ដើមលក់ផលិតផលកសិកម្មខ្មែរតាមអនឡាញ ក្រោមឈ្មោះ «ស្រុកស្រែ អនឡាញ»។ ខ្ញុំចង់សុំពេលបងប្រហែល ៥ នាទី ដើម្បីពិភាក្សាអំពីការយកផលិតផលរបស់បងមកលក់។ តើបងទំនេរនិយាយបន្តិចបានទេបង?

*English:* Hello! Sorry to bother you. I saw your banana flour on Facebook and already bought a pack to try; it tastes good and the packaging is nice. I am starting to sell Khmer agricultural products online under the name "Srok Srae Online." Could I have about 5 minutes to discuss selling your product?

(For cashews, replace ម្សៅចេក with គ្រាប់ស្វាយចន្ទី. For lotus root powder, use ម្សៅឈូក.)

**A2. Understand their situation first (ask, then listen)**

> មុននឹងខ្ញុំនិយាយពីគម្រោងរបស់ខ្ញុំ ខ្ញុំសុំសួរបងបន្តិចសិន៖
> ១. សព្វថ្ងៃបងលក់ឲ្យនរណាខ្លះ? លក់នៅហាង ផ្សារ ឬមានអ្នកយកទៅលក់បន្ត?
> ២. មួយខែៗ បងផលិតបានប្រហែលប៉ុន្មានកញ្ចប់? ហើយលក់ដាច់ប្រហែលប៉ុន្មាន?
> ៣. អ្វីដែលពិបាកជាងគេសម្រាប់បងពេលនេះ គឺការផលិត ឬការរកអ្នកទិញ?

*English:* Before I explain my plan, may I ask: (1) Who do you sell to now: shop, market, resellers? (2) How many packs do you produce and sell per month? (3) What is harder for you right now, producing or finding buyers?

**A3. The pitch**

> ខ្ញុំដឹងថាបងពូកែខាងផលិតណាស់ ហើយខ្ញុំមិនចង់ទៅរៀនផលិតប្រកួតជាមួយបងទេ។ អ្វីដែលខ្ញុំអាចជួយបង គឺផ្នែកលក់អនឡាញទាំងអស់៖ ខ្ញុំថតវីដេអូ TikTok និងហ្វេសប៊ុក ឆ្លើយឆាតជាមួយអតិថិជន វេចខ្ចប់ ផ្ញើទៅតាមខេត្ត ហើយថ្ងៃក្រោយទៀតផ្ញើទៅបងប្អូនខ្មែរនៅបរទេសផងដែរ។ ខ្ញុំជាអ្នកចំណាយលុយផ្សាយពាណិជ្ជកម្មខ្លួនឯង។ បងគ្រាន់តែរក្សាគុណភាពឲ្យល្អដូចសព្វថ្ងៃ។ រាល់វីដេអូ ខ្ញុំនឹងប្រាប់អតិថិជនច្បាស់ថាផលិតផលនេះមកពីសិប្បកម្មរបស់បង នៅខេត្តណា ដូច្នេះឈ្មោះរបស់បងក៏កាន់តែល្បីដែរ។

*English:* You are skilled at production, and I don't want to compete with you on that. I handle all online selling: TikTok and Facebook videos, customer chat, packing, provincial shipping, and later shipping to Cambodians overseas. I pay for ads myself. You just keep the quality as it is. In every video I will tell customers clearly the product comes from your workshop and province, so your name grows too.

**A4. The offer (60-day trial)**

> ខ្ញុំសុំសាកល្បងជាមួយបងរយៈពេល ៦០ ថ្ងៃសិន។ លើកដំបូង ខ្ញុំសុំយក ៣០ កញ្ចប់ ក្នុងតម្លៃលក់ដុំ។ ខ្ញុំបង់លុយពាក់កណ្ដាលភ្លាមៗពេលមកយកទំនិញ ហើយពាក់កណ្ដាលទៀតខ្ញុំបង់ក្នុងរយៈពេល ១៤ ថ្ងៃ តាម ABA។ ឬបើបងយល់ព្រម យើងធ្វើជាផ្ញើលក់ ខ្ញុំទូទាត់តាមចំនួនលក់ដាច់រៀងរាល់ ២ សប្ដាហ៍ម្ដង ហើយបើនៅសល់ ខ្ញុំសងទំនិញវិញក្នុងសភាពនៅបិទជិតដដែល។ រៀងរាល់ចុងសប្ដាហ៍ ខ្ញុំផ្ញើរបាយការណ៍លក់ឲ្យបងមើលក្នុង Telegram។

*English:* I'd like a 60-day trial. First order: 30 packs at wholesale price. I pay half on pickup and half within 14 days via ABA. Or, if you agree, consignment: I pay for sold units every 2 weeks and return unsold units still sealed. Every weekend I send you a sales report on Telegram.

**A5. Questions to ask before closing (write the answers in the sheet)**

> បងអាចប្រាប់ខ្ញុំបន្តិចបានទេ៖
> ១. តម្លៃលក់ដុំក្នុងមួយកញ្ចប់ប៉ុន្មាន? បើយក ៣០ កញ្ចប់ ៥០ កញ្ចប់ ឬ ១០០ កញ្ចប់ តម្លៃខុសគ្នាទេ?
> ២. តម្លៃលក់រាយដែលបងលក់នៅហាងប៉ុន្មាន? ខ្ញុំនឹងលក់តាមតម្លៃនេះដែរ មិនលក់ថោកជាងបងទេ។
> ៣. អាយុកាលប្រើប្រាស់រយៈពេលប៉ុន្មានខែ? គួររក្សាទុកយ៉ាងម៉េច?
> ៤. មានលិខិតអនុញ្ញាត ឬវិញ្ញាបនបត្រពីក្រសួងណាមួយទេ? ខ្ញុំសុំថតរូបទុកផង។
> ៥. បើខ្ញុំកុម្ម៉ង់ថ្ងៃនេះ ប៉ុន្មានថ្ងៃទើបបងផ្ញើមកដល់ភ្នំពេញ?
> ៦. ខ្ញុំអាចមកថតវីដេអូពេលបងកំពុងផលិតបានទេ?

*English:* (1) Wholesale price per pack? Different at 30, 50, 100 packs? (2) Your shop retail price? I will sell at this price, never cheaper. (3) Shelf life and storage? **A6. Asking for online exclusivity (say this at the end of the visit, or at Day 45)**

### Script C: Agricultural Input Supplier, wholesale terms now and white-label (OEM) later

**C1. Opening (message or call to the sales manager)**

> ជម្រាបសួរលោក/លោកស្រី! ខ្ញុំកំពុងបើកហាងលក់ជីកសិកម្មតាមអនឡាញ ក្រោមឈ្មោះ «ស្រុកស្រែ អនឡាញ» ផ្ដោតលើកសិករនៅកំពង់ចាម ត្បូងឃ្មុំ បាត់ដំបង និងពោធិ៍សាត់។ ខ្ញុំចាប់អារម្មណ៍លើផលិតផលជីរបស់ក្រុមហ៊ុនលោក ហើយចង់ក្លាយជាដីលឺលក់អនឡាញ។ ខ្ញុំសុំតារាងតម្លៃដីលឺ និងព័ត៌មានលក្ខខណ្ឌសម្រាប់ដីលឺថ្មីបានទេ?

*English:* Hello! I'm opening an online agricultural fertilizer store, "Srok Srae Online," focused on farmers in Kampong Cham, Tboung Khmum, Battambang and Pursat. I'm interested in your fertilizers and want to become an online dealer. May I have your dealer price list and new-dealer terms?

**C2. Legitimacy questions (ask before price)**

> មុននឹងនិយាយពីតម្លៃ ខ្ញុំសុំសួរបន្តិច៖
> ១. ផលិតផលទាំងអស់មានលេខចុះបញ្ជីពីក្រសួងកសិកម្ម រុក្ខាប្រមាញ់ និងនេសាទហើយឬនៅ? ខ្ញុំសុំថតរូបលេខចុះបញ្ជី និងស្លាកផលិតផលទុកផង។
> ២. ក្រុមហ៊ុនមានអាជ្ញាប័ណ្ណអាជីវកម្មសម្ភារៈកសិកម្មទេ?
> ៣. ទំនិញដែលនឹងផ្ញើឲ្យខ្ញុំ ផលិតខែណា ហើយផុតកំណត់ពេលណា?
> ៤. ក្រុមហ៊ុនមានទិន្នន័យសាកល្បងនៅចម្ការ ឬលេខទូរស័ព្ទកសិករ ២ នាក់ ដែលប្រើយូរហើយ ឲ្យខ្ញុំសាកសួរបានទេ?

*English:* (1) Do all products have MAFF registration numbers? May I photograph them and the labels? (2) Does the company have an agricultural materials business license? (3) Manufacturing and expiry dates of the stock I'd receive? (4) Any field trial data, or 2 long-time farmer customers I can call?

> ៣. ក្រុមហ៊ុនមានកំណត់តម្លៃលក់រាយ សម្រាប់ដីលឺទាំងអស់ទេ? ខ្ញុំចង់ដឹងព្រោះមិនចង់ប្រកួតបញ្ចុះតម្លៃជាមួយដីលឺផ្សេងទៀតនៅលើអនឡាញ។
> ៤. បើទំនិញខូច លេច ឬជិតផុតកំណត់ ក្រុមហ៊ុនប្ដូរឲ្យទេ?

*English:* I plan to start with 3 SKUs: 1 L liquid foliar, 5 kg bio-organic, 1 kg fruit-tree fertilizer. (1) Dealer price at $200, $500, $1,000 orders? (2) Minimum order; can I mix SKUs to reach it? **C4. Negotiation asks (pick 2 to 3, not all)**

> ខ្ញុំសុំលោកជួយខ្ញុំក្នុងនាមជាដីលឺថ្មី៖
